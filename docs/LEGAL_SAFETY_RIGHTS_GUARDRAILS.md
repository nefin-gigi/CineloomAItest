# Legal, Safety, and Rights Guardrails

Before public paid launch, CineLoom should enforce:

- User confirmation that they own or have permission to use uploaded scripts.
- Commercial-use policy acknowledgement for paid exports.
- Public figure / likeness review path.
- Brand/copyrighted style avoidance guidance.
- Content moderation for prohibited content.
- Takedown and dispute workflow.
- Data deletion and retention controls.
- Audit trail for generation/export actions.

## Product UX

Add a rights confirmation checkbox before upload:

"I confirm I own this script or have permission to process and generate storyboard assets from it."
