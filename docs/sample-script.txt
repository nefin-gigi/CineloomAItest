EXT. DESERT RIDGE — SUNSET

Moses walks alone across a ridge, staff in hand. The sky burns gold, but his face carries the weight of doubt.

MOSES (V.O.)
I am not the one you seek.

The wind rises. Sand moves around his feet. He stops, listening.

EXT. BURNING BUSH — TWILIGHT

A flame appears in the distance. It burns, but the bush is not consumed. Moses steps closer, afraid and drawn forward at the same time.
