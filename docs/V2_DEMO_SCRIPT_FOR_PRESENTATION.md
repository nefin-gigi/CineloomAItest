Open the dashboard. Point to the customer score ribbon and the three key actions: Play Guided Demo, Review Storyboard, Export Package.

## 1:30–3:30 — Guided demo
Open `/studio/investor-demo`. Click Play Guided Demo. Explain that CineLoom is not only a prompt box; it is a structured film pre-production engine.

## 3:30–4:30 — Storyboard proof
Click through the premium storyboard frames. Use the quick revision buttons: "Make the close-up more emotional" and "Keep Moses consistent."

## 4:30–5:30 — Animatic
Play the animatic simulator and show panel timing, dialogue, music, and SFX.

## 5:30–6:30 — QA and export
Show the QA score and click Generate Export Preview.

## 6:30–7:00 — Close
Position CineLoom as a script-to-storyboard-to-animatic-to-video-handoff studio for short films, trailers, pitch decks, producers, and indie creators.
