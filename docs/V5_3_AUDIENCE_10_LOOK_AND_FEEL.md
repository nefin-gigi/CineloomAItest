# CineLoom v5.3 — Audience 10/10 Look and Feel

This release improves the site experience for every primary cinema and SaaS audience while keeping the interface simple, legible, and mobile-first.

## Goals

- Make the homepage feel premium enough to compare with top modern SaaS platforms.
- Make the visual language relevant to cinema audiences without returning to a cluttered dark theme.
- Give each audience a clear 10/10 route: creators, YouTube/Reels creators, film students, indie filmmakers, producers, and studios.
- Improve the perception of product proof with a clearer script-to-export story.
- Preserve the v5.2 mobile app-style dock, sticky navigation, and safe-area spacing.

## Added

- `Audience10Experience` homepage section.
- New `audience-10.css` visual polish layer.
- Audience score strip with clarity, mobile, cinema relevance, and SaaS trust goals.
- Horizontal mobile workflow rail: Script → Beats → Shots → Panels → Animatic → Export.
- Six audience cards with tailored promise, proof tags, and workflow links.
- More premium shadows, spacing, gradients, hover states, and storyboard-panel treatment.
- Reduced-motion support for motion-sensitive users.
